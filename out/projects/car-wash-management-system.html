Overview

A car wash management system built with a type-safe backend to handle daily operations, customer records and business reporting.

The Challenge

What problem needed to be solved

Car wash businesses handle a high volume of daily transactions, customer visits and service records. Without a centralized system, tracking service history, managing staff schedules, reconciling payments and understanding business performance becomes a manual and time-consuming process.

The Solution

How the system addresses it

A business management system designed to organize customer information, service records, transactions and operational data in one place. The system provides a structured way to manage daily operations, track business activity and maintain records that support better decision-making.

Key Features

What the system includes

Operations
  • Service catalog management
  • Job creation and tracking
  • Staff assignment
  • Queue and workflow management
Customer Management
  • Customer registration and profiles
  • Service history
  • Vehicle records
Transactions
  • Payment recording
  • Transaction history
  • Daily sales tracking
Reporting
  • Daily operations summary
  • Revenue reports
  • Service frequency analysis
How It Works

From input to output

01

Service Request

A customer service request is created, selecting the required services and vehicle details.

02

The system assigns the job, tracks progress and updates the service status.

03

Completion & Payment

Once the service is complete, payment is recorded and the transaction is finalized.

04

Recording

All data is persisted, updating customer history and operational records.
